# Hi, I'm Tate Zhang 👋

I'm building **[Nuvoca](https://nuvocabi.com)** — booking and AI reception for small, appointment-based shops in the US: massage spas, nail salons, barbershops and hair salons.

**What Nuvoca does**

- 📅 **Free online booking on BookAround.** Every shop gets its own booking page and QR code on BookAround, our app for customers. Customers pick a service and a real open time, up to 90 days ahead, and the booking lands straight in the shop's calendar with no double bookings.
- 📱 **Free mobile dashboard.** Calendar, customers, hours and services, managed from a phone and installable like an app.
- 🤖 **Free AI shop assistant.** Owners type or talk in English or Chinese ("Closed next Friday afternoon for inventory") and the assistant drafts the change. Nothing happens until the owner taps *Confirm*.
- ☎️ **AI voice receptionist (paid).** Answers the shop's phone 24/7 in English and Chinese, answers questions about hours, prices and directions, and books callers into the same calendar.

Shop owners can open a free shop themselves in a few minutes at **[nuvocabi.com/signup](https://nuvocabi.com/signup)**.

**How it's built**

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `React` · `Google Cloud Run` · `Firestore` · `Firebase Hosting` · `Twilio (Voice, SMS, Verify)` · `OpenAI Realtime & Responses API` · `PWA`

Things I care about: software a busy shop owner can trust without reading a manual, bilingual by default, and AI that drafts while people decide.

**Find me**

- 🌐 Website: [nuvocabi.com](https://nuvocabi.com)
- 📝 Blog: [nuvocabi.com/blog](https://nuvocabi.com/blog/en/)
